# Repository map

A plain-English guide to every folder of this repository (layout of v0.2.0). Nothing here is a
rule of the verifier: it only tells you where things are and how much each one matters for trust.

## The three kinds of content

1. **The verifier** is what you trust when `janus verify` says "Verified". It is small on purpose:
   `Janus/` (the Lean framework: the ISA models, the contracts, the auditor), `cmd/janus/` (the Go
   command that extracts, drives Lean, sandboxes the proof and checks the result), the two root
   Go files that embed the framework into the binary, and the two ISA-model packages in `models/`
   that hold the kernel-checked agreement proofs with Arm's ASL and the Sail x86 model (used by
   `janus verify --asl`). The soundness argument, and exactly what is and is not trusted, is
   `docs/SOUNDNESS.md`.
2. **Reference validation** (`validation/`) tests the *ISA models* against real hardware and against
   independent reference models. It is **evidence, not proof**: it raises confidence that the
   models describe the machines, but no verification result depends on it at run time.
3. **Tests and red team** (`test/`, plus `examples/` and `bench/`) try to break or measure the
   verifier. Red-team rounds are attack files with pinned expectations; the gate fails if any
   attack starts to pass or a rejection changes its reason.

## Source versus generated

* **Source (tracked):** everything above. Vendored third-party models are tracked as tarballs
  (`models/asl/vendor/`, `models/x86/vendor/`) with recorded SHA-256 sums; the Sail-generated Lean
  parts (`models/*/ArmASL*`, `X86Sail*`) are produced from them by `scripts/build/asl.sh` and
  `scripts/build/x86formal.sh` and are not tracked.
* **Generated, never tracked:** `.lake/` (Lake builds), `artifacts/` (example binaries, extractions
  and verification reports), `bin/`, `test/redteam/**/bin|work*` (red-team scratch), comparison
  outputs, and the result/olean caches under `JANUS_CACHE_DIR`. They are safe to delete; the
  verifier rebuilds them.

## Entry points

* **CLI:** `janus verify <binary> <symbol> --proof <file> ...`, `janus extract`, `janus list`,
  `janus verify-batch`, `janus init`, `janus explain`, `janus blockers` (`docs/CLI.md`).
* **Build:** `go build -trimpath -buildvcs=false ./cmd/janus`; Lake targets `Janus`, `JanusTests`,
  `janus-audit` (the auditor), `janus-decode`, and the validation executables
  (`janus-oracle`, `janus-oracle-amd64`, `janus-testfloat`).
* **Merge gate:** `scripts/gate/check-merge.sh` (verify, Go tests, red-team rounds, axiom guard,
  vet); the ISA-model lanes via `scripts/gate/gate.sh --only ...` (`docs/TESTING.md`).
* **Verification of the examples:** `scripts/verify/verify-all-examples.sh` (and the per-kind
  `verify-*-examples.sh`); build them with `scripts/build/build-examples*.sh`.
* **Red team:** `test/redteam/run-all.sh`, `test/redteam/run-rounds.sh`, one round with
  `test/redteam/rounds/roundN/run-roundN.sh`.
* **Reference validation:** `scripts/validation/hardware-arm64.sh`, `hardware-amd64.sh`,
  `sail-arm.sh`, `testfloat.sh`, `softfloat-check.sh`, `decoder-coverage.sh`; ISA-model builds
  `scripts/build/asl.sh` and `scripts/build/x86formal.sh`.

`scripts/` is split into `gate/` (merge gate, gate libraries and their self-tests), `build/`
(example and model builds), `verify/` (example verification), `validation/` (reference-validation
runners), `dev/` (benchmarks, coverage, generators still in use) and `archive/` (one-shot
generators kept for history).
